Stay in Demand by Staying Visible: One Weekly Brief for Executives 50+

Practical brand moves for leaders 50+ to create options for themselves, before they need them.

The market has shifted.

A strong resume and glowing references are no longer enough. Decision-makers increasingly vet leaders through search results and public online signals.

At the same time, age bias is rising. A 2025 survey cited by Forbes found that 99% of workers over 40 reported experiencing ageism at work*.

Facing these headwinds, senior leaders can't rely on tenure, reputation, or past roles alone to speak for themselves.

The good news: You can take control of your professional future by building a strong digital signal today.

It all starts here.
