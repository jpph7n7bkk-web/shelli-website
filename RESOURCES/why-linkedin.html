6 Reasons Why I Choose LinkedIn™ for My Business (and Why It Matters for Yours Too)

By Shelli K. Spence  ·  Shelli's Socials

Have you ever wondered if LinkedIn™ is really the right place for you?

I have. More than one person has hinted I'd grow faster elsewhere. I have several friends exploding on TikTok, landing sponsorships and brand deals. But after careful research and hands-on experience, I know LinkedIn™ is the place for my business — and quite possibly for yours too.

Here's why.

Reason 1

Every Engagement Is a Serious Marketing Move

Your name, headline, and custom link (for Premium members) follow you everywhere. When your headline clearly states who you help, what pain you solve, and how you solve it, every comment operates as a billboard for your brand. What other platform gives you this kind of free advertising?

Reason 2

LinkedIn™ Requires Real Identity

On LinkedIn™, your real identity is the product. Your name, job history, company, connections, and credentials are the value LinkedIn sells to recruiters, employers, advertisers, and businesses. This means it's in LinkedIn's best interest to require true identity — giving you a dramatically higher chance of knowing exactly who you're talking to compared to entertainment-driven platforms.

Reason 3

Premium Users Can See Who Viewed Their Profile

If you're serious about your business, this is serious data. Profile views are buying signals — indicators that someone is curious about your offer. When your content and social selling strategy are functioning properly, you should be getting more views each day than you can keep up with in your outreach.

Reason 4

Your Optimized Profile Is a Conversion Machine

Nowhere else gives you this much space to showcase yourself as "the solution." That's why I emphasize profile optimization with my clients before anything else. An optimized profile converts visitors into connections, newsletter subscribers, or booked calls — passively, 24/7.

Reason 5

Your Profile and Articles Are Indexed by Search Engines — Including AI

This is a massively underrated advantage. You can show up in results without spending a dime. No other social media platform offers this. Your content can be discovered by Google and AI search engines, which means visibility that compounds over time.

Reason 6 (Bonus)

Less Competition = Faster Growth

Despite the complaints that "LinkedIn is becoming Facebook," it remains the top networking platform for professionals — the closest thing in the world to a global professional directory. Under 2% of all members publish content, so there is still enormous room for growth compared to entertainment platforms. It's still early.

I spend time on Instagram and TikTok to be entertained.
I'm on LinkedIn™ because I mean business.

The professionals who thrive on LinkedIn™ aren't the loudest ones or the most frequent posters. They're the ones who show up consistently, communicate their value clearly, and let the platform work for them.
